About Dalecrest

Dalecrest is a medieval village that lives on whether or not anyone is watching. Its residents work, eat, make friends, hold moots and remember what happens to them, and an AI model chooses what each of them does next from what they know. The village keeps time of its own, a little faster than ours, and grows as families arrive and new buildings go up.

Key

  • A resident out of doors.
  • The resident you have selected. When they are indoors, a ring marks their window.
  • ?A resident deciding what to do next.

Moving about

  • Drag to pan. Click the map, then scroll to zoom, or use + and −.
  • ⌂ shows the whole village again, and the corner button below it fills the screen.
  • Click a resident to open their record.
