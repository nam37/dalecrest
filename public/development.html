27 November 2026

Kinder faces, company from pets and a lighter page

Resident portraits have kinder faces. Each person is drawn from one of six face shapes with rounder noses and lighter eyes, and keeps the same face from visit to visit. Cats and dogs now keep their households company: a resident at home with a pet grows lonely more slowly, though only people ease it. The bridge on the map now reaches both banks of the river.

The village page sends about a third as much on a first visit, and its scripts download again only when they change. A resident’s Data tab still shows their whole record. Residents are asked to reconsider their plans less often as a moot approaches, and resident cards now open while you watch the village in full screen.

When several residents need to decide at the same moment, as most do once a village day, their choices are now made side by side instead of one at a time. The last in line used to stand waiting for close to half a minute and should now wait only a few seconds. Goal reviews now stay within their hourly spending limit. The roadmap plans a very rare find of gold for residents fishing from the bank.

26 November 2026

The first family arrives

Maud and William Barrow came from Ashby with their four children, a cart and two oxen. They’re lodging in the barn while they wait for a cottage. The mill wheel now turns on the village map.

25 November 2026

Robin’s Journal, pets and evening moots

Robin’s Journal opened with dated entries and a catalog for his songs. Cats and dogs can join households, children chase each other on the green, and residents carry loads and tools you can see in Map and Walk.

Resident cards gained a pin. Newly scheduled moots moved to 7 p.m. after fixes to how residents set out and are counted. The Daily archive gained search and a wider selection of woodcuts.

24 November 2026

Woodcuts for the Daily and new resident portraits

Dalecrest Daily gained woodcut illustrations for lead articles and archive controls above the story. Resident portraits now stay with each person as the village roster changes. The clock gained a sun-and-moon dial and a pace that can run faster at night.

23 November 2026

More room for the village map

The live map gained a full-screen view. Resident cards now explain when someone changes course because the person they were going to visit moved.

22 November 2026

Records, following and the common store

Residents, households, promises and moots gained shareable record pages. You can follow those records in your browser. The village gained a Storehouse and its daily bread allowance, and residents can carry goals forward and reconsider them as they learn.

21 November 2026

Dalecrest opens

The observatory opened with a live map, a walking view and a chronicle. Dalecrest Daily began publishing village news from Robin Marsh’s reporting rounds. The Sandbox lets you try a separate village in your browser.
